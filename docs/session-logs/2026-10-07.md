# Session log — 7 October 2026

A running record of every change we make to hapmap.app during this session.
Each entry says what changed, why, and how to undo it if need be.

## Where we started

The repo was clean on `main` at `bbe3593` (perf(map): match raster tileSize to COG block size, 21 September 2026).
Production is the older Leaflet site; the MapLibre work lives on the `hapmap-dev` test Worker.

## Changes

### Took stock of the R2 bucket

Nothing on the site or in the bucket changed.

We listed the bucket and compared both deployed pages with the repo.
Production serves `index.html` at `bbe3593` exactly, with 1955 and the SOC276 1980 layer.
Dev serves the SOC195 1980 layer and a 1926 layer instead.
`https://hapmap.app` reset the connection from hectar, so production was checked through its `workers.dev` address.

The findings went into [docs/r2-inventory.md](../r2-inventory.md), a register of every object in the bucket: its size, where it came from, and where it is shown.
It also lists the five new Blag Map mosaics that are waiting to go in.

### Planned the clean-up and the upgrade

Nothing on the site or in the bucket changed.

[docs/mosaic-upgrade-plan.md](../mosaic-upgrade-plan.md) sets out four decisions for bilby and five steps.
The steps delete 2.5 GB that nothing reads, upload the Blag Map mosaics, try them on dev, promote them, and then retire the layers they replace.
The steps went into `backlog.md` as tasks.

### Uploaded five new mosaics to R2

bilby agreed the plan.
The five Blag Map mosaics were reprojected to EPSG:3857 COGs in `~/hap-mosaics/derived/` and uploaded under new `*-blag-3857.tif` keys, each checked byte for byte.
The 1980 COG was then rebuilt with an alpha band, because without one its edges would draw black, and re-uploaded under the same key before anything had loaded it.

To undo: delete the five `cog/*-blag-3857.tif` keys.

### Put the new years on the dev site

`dev/index.html` gained 1944, 1945 and 1974–75, and swapped its 1926 and 1980 layers for the Blag Map ones.
A `cogYear()` helper builds each COG layer, and an optional `label` lets the handle read "1974–75".
All the GA layers now share one credit string, so MapLibre shows it once.
Deployed to `hapmap-dev` (version `7fd62747`).

Every layer was loaded in headless Chromium at overview and street zoom, beside the 2025 imagery.
None threw an error, every edge was transparent, and 1980 and 1974–75 sit squarely on today's roads and bridges.

To undo: `dev/` is gitignored, so roll back with `npx wrangler rollback` on `hapmap-dev`.

### Promoted the new years to production

`index.html` now carries the same layers as dev, without dev's banner or ACTmapi layers, and the timeline reaches back to 1920.
The README was corrected: it had claimed the COGs were reprojected on the GPU from UTM, and listed only three years.
Deployed to the `hapmap` Worker (version `cf2df24c`) and checked the same way: all six historical years render, with no errors.

**The public 1980 layer is no longer about 200 m out of place.**

To undo: `npx wrangler rollback` on `hapmap`, though the SOC276 COG the old page needs is now deleted (below).

- `cog/canberra-1926-map3584-3857.tif` (0.01 GB), the preview-frame 1926 layer.
- `north-canberra-1955/` (1.23 GB, 100,966 tiles), the Leaflet-era tile pyramid.
- `cog/north-canberra-1955-odm-3857-jpeg.tif` (0.72 GB) and `cog/north-canberra-1955-cac80-large.tif` (0.57 GB), two retired 1955 COGs.

The last three were copied to `~/hap-mosaics/retired-from-r2/` before deletion; bilby said afterwards that backups are not needed.

The SOC195 1980 COG stays for now: it is sharper than the new 1980 layer, and goes when a full-scan Blag Map 1980 exists.
